Discover Pula and Istria with Us – Your Complete Vacation Experience!

We are a dedicated team passionate about creating unforgettable summer holidays for guests staying in Istria. Our mission is to offer everything you need for a perfect getaway – from comfortable apartments and cozy bungalows to luxury yacht charters and adventurous boat excursions.

At Vacation Istria, we believe that true hospitality means taking care of every detail. That’s why our guests return year after year, drawn by the quality of service, warm atmosphere, and the unforgettable experiences we provide. Join us, and discover why we are one of the most trusted names in Istria!

Boat Tours & Excursions in Pula – Brijuni, Dolphins & More

Once you’re settled into your apartment or bungalow, it’s time to explore the stunning surroundings. One of the best ways to experience the beauty of Istria is by boat.

We offer daily boat excursions from Pula, including tours around the Brijuni Islands, and a special dolphin-watching tour during sunset. Choose between midday and evening trips, or enjoy both for a full day of discovery. With breathtaking views, swimming stops, and a chance to see dolphins in their natural habitat, these tours are a highlight for many of our guests. If you’re looking for even more freedom, try our private boat rentals or luxury yacht charters. Cruise at your own pace and discover hidden coves and islands near Pula. It’s the ultimate way to relax and enjoy the Adriatic Sea.

More Adventures: Kayak, Cave Swimming, Diving & Day Trips

For the more adventurous, we also organize: Kayak tours and cave swimming – explore the coast from a new perspective, paddle into sea caves, and swim in crystal-clear waters.

Individual diving experiences – suitable for beginners or certified divers, offering personal guidance and unforgettable underwater exploration. Day trips to Rovinj and Venice – board a fast boat for a full-day cultural escape. Discover the charm of Rovinj or the magic of Venice in just one day!
