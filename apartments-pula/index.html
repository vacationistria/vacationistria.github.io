FAQ

Where are your apartments located in Pula?

Our apartments are located in quiet residential areas of Pula, close to beaches, marinas, and the city center. Most attractions, restaurants, and shops are easily accessible within a short drive or walking distance.

How far are the apartments from the beach?

The nearest beaches are typically within 5–15 minutes by car or a short bike ride, depending on the apartment location. Pula offers a mix of rocky, pebble, and family-friendly beaches.

Is parking available at the apartments?

Yes, all our apartments include free private parking or nearby parking space, which is especially important during the busy summer season.

Do the apartments have WiFi and air conditioning?

Yes, all apartments are equipped with fast WiFi and air conditioning to ensure a comfortable stay during the hot summer months.

Are the apartments suitable for families?

Absolutely. Pula is a family-friendly destination with calm beaches, parks, and many activities. Our apartments are ideal for families, couples, and groups.

Can couples stay in your apartments?

Yes, many of our apartments are perfect for couples looking for a peaceful and relaxing holiday near the sea.

How far is Pula Airport from the apartments?

Pula Airport is located about 10–15 minutes by car from most apartments, making arrival quick and convenient.

Is a car necessary when staying in Pula?

A car is recommended if you want to explore the region (Rovinj, Brijuni, Cape Kamenjak), but local buses and taxis are also available.

Are shops and restaurants nearby?

Yes, there are supermarkets, local markets, restaurants, and cafes within walking distance or a short drive from the apartments.
