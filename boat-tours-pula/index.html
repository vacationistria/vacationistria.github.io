Boat Tours Pula – Discover the Beauty of the Adriatic Sea

If you are spending your holiday in Pula, one of the best experiences you can enjoy is a boat tour around the stunning coastline of Istria. Our boat tours Pula offer a unique opportunity to explore hidden bays, crystal-clear water, and the breathtaking islands of the Adriatic Sea. During this unforgettable boat trip in Pula, you will cruise around the famous Brijuni Islands, one of the most beautiful archipelagos in Croatia.

These islands are known for their rich nature, turquoise sea, and peaceful atmosphere, making them a perfect destination for relaxation and adventure. Whether you are visiting as a couple, with friends, or with your family, our boat excursion in Pula is designed to give you an authentic Adriatic experience.

Brijuni Boat Tour Experience

Our popular Brijuni boat tour from Pula lasts approximately 4.5 hours and offers spectacular panoramic views of the Adriatic coastline.

During the cruise you will:

  • Enjoy scenic views of the Brijuni archipelago

  • Relax with food and unlimited drinks

  • Learn interesting stories from our guide

  • Experience the beauty of the Adriatic Sea

One of the highlights of our boat tours in Pula is a stop at St. Jerolim Island, where guests can swim in crystal-clear water and enjoy the peaceful natural surroundings.

Day Boat Tour Pula (Swimming Stop)

The day boat tour in Pula starts at 12:00 and ends at 16:30. During this tour we make a panoramic cruise around the Brijuni Islands, followed by a two-hour swimming stop at St. Jerolim Island.

This option is perfect for:

  • families with children

  • swimming lovers

  • travelers who want to explore the Adriatic during the day

The relaxing atmosphere, delicious food, and beautiful scenery make this boat trip in Pula one of the most popular activities in Istria.

Sunset Boat Tour Pula & Dolphin Watching

Our sunset boat tour in Pula starts at 18:00 and ends at 21:00. This evening boat excursion in Pula offers a magical Adriatic experience. As the sun sets over the sea, guests can enjoy a relaxing cruise around the Brijuni Islands while watching dolphins in their natural habitat.

The dolphin watching boat tour in Pula is perfect for:

  • couples looking for a romantic sunset experience
  • photographers and nature lovers
  • travelers who want a peaceful evening on the sea

Watching dolphins during sunset is one of the most memorable moments you can experience on the Adriatic coast.

FAQ

1. What can I expect on a Boat Tour in Pula?

The boat tour offers a scenic cruise around the Brijuni Islands (about 4.5 hours by day, about 3 hours at sunset) with food, unlimited drinks, and either swimming (day tour) or dolphin watching at sunset (evening tour).

2. How long does the boat tour last?

The day tour lasts about 4.5 hours (12:00–16:30) and the sunset tour about 3 hours (18:00–21:00), including cruising, relaxation, and meal service.

3. What is the difference between the Day Tour and the Sunset Tour?

Day Tour (12:00 – 16:30):

Includes a 2-hour swimming stop at St. Jerolim

Ideal for families and swimming lovers

Sunset Tour (18:00 – 21:00):

No swimming stop

Focus on sunset views and dolphin watching

Romantic and relaxed atmosphere

4. Is food included in the Boat Tour?

Yes. Each guest can choose one meal:

Fish

Burger

Vegetarian option

Drinks (wine, juices, mineral water) are unlimited during the tour.

5. How much does the Boat Tour cost?

Adults: 50 €

Children under 3 years: Free

6. Do I need to pay extra for the swimming stop?

Yes, during the Day Tour there is a small entrance fee for St. Jerolim Island (part of Brijuni National Park), payable locally.

7. Are dolphins guaranteed on the sunset tour?

While dolphins are wild animals and sightings cannot be guaranteed, they are frequently seen during evening tours and are one of the most memorable parts of the experience.

8. Is the boat tour suitable for children?

Yes, both day and sunset tours are suitable for families. Children especially enjoy swimming stops and dolphin sightings.

9. Can I bring my pet on the boat tour?

Yes, pets are allowed on board.

10. Where does the Boat Tour start?

Departure is from the Riva in Pula, near ACI Marina. Guests should arrive at least 30 minutes before departure.

11. What should I bring on the tour?

For the Day Tour:

Swimsuit

Towel

Sunscreen

Snorkeling equipment (optional)

For the Sunset Tour:

Light jacket (in case of wind)

Camera for sunset and dolphins

12. Is the Boat Tour good for special occasions?

Yes. The boat tour is a great option for:

Romantic sunset evenings

Group vacations

Summer celebrations

Relaxed sea experiences
