About Dolphins in the Adriatic Sea

Dolphins living in the Adriatic Sea are highly intelligent and social marine mammals, most commonly bottlenose dolphins. They live in groups called pods and are known for their playful behavior, often swimming alongside boats and interacting with their environment. Dolphins use sound and echolocation to communicate and navigate, which makes them incredibly skilled hunters. In the waters around Pula and the Brijuni Islands, dolphins are frequently seen, especially during the evening hours when they are more active.

This is why dolphin watching tours in Pula are scheduled around sunset, offering the best chance to experience these amazing animals in their natural habitat. Although dolphins are wild animals and sightings cannot be guaranteed, they are very common in this area, making Pula one of the best places in Croatia for dolphin watching.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dolphin Tour Pula

What is included in the Dolphin Tour in Pula?

The Dolphin Tour includes a 3-hour sunset boat trip, dolphin watching near the Brijuni Islands, dinner on board (fish, burger or vegetarian), and unlimited drinks such as wine, water, and soft drinks.

How long does the Dolphin Tour last?

The tour lasts approximately 3 hours, usually from around 18:00 to 21:00, depending on the sunset time.

Where does the Dolphin Tour start?

The Dolphin Tour starts from the harbor in Pula, near the ACI Marina on the city waterfront.

Is seeing dolphins guaranteed on the tour?

No. Dolphins are wild animals in their natural habitat, so sightings cannot be guaranteed, but the chances are very high during sunset hours around the Brijuni Islands.

What kind of dolphins can be seen near Pula?

The most common species in this area is the bottlenose dolphin, which frequently appears in the waters around the Brijuni archipelago.

Is dinner included on the dolphin watching tour?

Yes. Guests can choose between fish, burger or vegetarian dinner, and drinks such as wine, water, and juices are usually unlimited during the trip.

Is the Dolphin Tour suitable for children?

Yes. The tour is family-friendly and safe for children. Children under 3 travel free.

Are pets allowed on the boat?

Yes. Pets are welcome on board, as long as they are supervised by their owners.

What should I bring on the Dolphin Tour?

It is recommended to bring a light jacket, a camera or phone for photos, and comfortable clothing because evenings at sea can be cooler.

When is the best time to see dolphins in Pula?

The best time is during sunset, when dolphins are often active and feeding near the Brijuni Islands.

Do we stop for swimming during the dolphin tour?

No. The sunset dolphin tour focuses on dolphin watching and enjoying the sunset, so there is usually no swimming stop.

How many people can the boat accommodate?

It depends on the boat, but the boats we work with are spacious, so there is always enough room at the tables. Just tell us how many of you are coming when you book.

Do I need to book the dolphin tour in advance?

Yes. During the summer season, dolphin tours in Pula are very popular and often sell out, so advance booking is recommended.
