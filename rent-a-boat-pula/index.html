FAQ

How can I rent a boat in Pula?

You can rent a boat in Pula by contacting us directly through our booking form, WhatsApp, or email. Choose your preferred date, boat type, and number of people, and we will confirm availability and pricing. Early booking is recommended during the summer season.

Do I need a license to rent a boat in Pula?

Yes, a valid boating license is required in Croatia to operate most motorboats. If you do not have a license, you can rent a boat in Pula with a professional skipper who will handle navigation for you.

Can I rent a boat in Pula without a license?

Yes. We offer boat rental in Pula with skipper service, which means you can enjoy the experience without needing a boating license. This is a popular option for tourists who want a relaxed and safe trip.

How much does boat rental in Pula cost?

Boat rental prices in Pula depend on the boat model, rental duration, and season. Prices typically vary between half-day and full-day rentals. Contact us for an exact quote tailored to your preferred date.

What is included in the boat rental price?

The price usually includes the boat, mandatory safety equipment, life jackets, and a short briefing before departure. Fuel and skipper service may be charged separately, depending on the selected option.

Where can I go with a rented boat from Pula?

With a rented boat from Pula, you can explore the stunning Brijuni National Park, Cape Kamenjak, hidden coves, secluded beaches, and beautiful islands along the Istrian coast. Renting a boat gives you full freedom to discover places inaccessible by car.

Is fuel included in the boat rental?

Fuel is usually not included in the base rental price. The final fuel cost depends on the distance traveled and engine consumption.

How long can I rent a boat in Pula?

You can rent a boat for half a day, a full day, or multiple days. Full-day boat rental in Pula is the most popular option, allowing enough time to explore islands and swim in crystal-clear waters.

What happens in case of bad weather?

If weather conditions are unsafe, the rental can be rescheduled or canceled without penalty. Safety is always our priority.

Why choose boat rental in Pula instead of a boat tour?

Renting a boat in Pula gives you privacy, flexibility, and complete control over your itinerary. Unlike standard boat tours, you decide where to stop, how long to swim, and which destinations to visit.
