Where the bungalow is located in Pula

Our bungalow is located in a quiet residential part of Pula, one of the most beautiful coastal towns in the region of Istria. The area is known for its relaxed Mediterranean atmosphere, pine trees, and crystal-clear sea. From here you can easily reach some of the most popular places in the city. The historic Pula Arena and the charming old town are only a short drive away, where you can enjoy restaurants, cafés, and ancient Roman landmarks.

The bungalow is also conveniently located near one of the main nautical hubs of the city, Marina Veruda, a large marina with hundreds of berths for yachts and sailing boats. For guests arriving by plane, Pula Airport is only about 10 km away, which means the transfer to the bungalow usually takes around 15 minutes by car. This location makes it easy to enjoy both sides of Pula – peaceful seaside living and vibrant city life.

Beaches near the bungalow

The southern part of Pula is famous for its rocky coves and small beaches surrounded by pine forests. Some of the best swimming spots in the city are just a short drive away, including Ambrela Beach and the beautiful coves around Verudela Peninsula. These beaches are known for their exceptionally clear water and natural shade from Mediterranean pine trees. Many visitors love them because they are perfect for swimming, snorkeling, or simply relaxing by the sea.

Marina and nautical life

Pula has a long maritime tradition, and sailing is part of everyday life here. Nearby Marina Veruda is one of the most important marinas in southern Istria and a popular starting point for sailing trips along the Adriatic coast. Because of this, the area often attracts sailors, divers, and travelers exploring the coastline by boat.

A bungalow is a type of accommodation that typically refers to a small, single-story house or cottage. In hospitality (such as at resorts or vacation rentals), a bungalow often means:

One-story detached unit: Usually freestanding, offering more privacy.

Compact but functional layout: Includes a bedroom, bathroom, and sometimes a small kitchen or living area.

Often in scenic or natural settings: Commonly found near beaches, in tropical resorts, or countryside retreats.

Private outdoor space: Many have verandas, patios, or small gardens.

It’s a popular option for travelers seeking comfort, privacy, and a relaxed atmosphere.

Size: Small to medium.

Floors: Always one-story.

Location: Often near beaches or tropical areas.

Style: Simple, cozy, with a casual vibe.

Ideal for: Couples, solo travelers, or small families wanting privacy and easy access to nature.

Things to do near the bungalow!

One of the best things about staying in Pula is the variety of activities available along the coast.

Boat tours! Boat excursions are one of the most popular activities in the area. Many visitors explore the coastline, hidden caves, and nearby islands by boat.

Dolphin watching! Sunset dolphin watching tours have become a favorite experience in Pula. Dolphins are often seen swimming in the waters of the Adriatic, especially during the evening hours.

Kayaking! Kayaking tours along the coast allow visitors to explore caves, cliffs, and hidden bays that are difficult to reach from land.

Brijuni Islands! A short boat ride away lies the beautiful Brijuni National Park, a group of islands famous for their nature, wildlife, and archaeological sites. Many visitors take day trips there from the nearby port of Fažana.
